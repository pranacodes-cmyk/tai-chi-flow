Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

TaiChi Flow is a guided Tai Chi, Qigong, chair exercise, walking and meditation app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: your answers, health details and practice history stay on your phone; a small set of app events is shared for ad measurement only when your privacy choice allows it; purchases are handled by Apple or Google with RevenueCat.

Information that stays on your device

Your onboarding answers (such as age range, goals, body measurements, activity level, comfort with balance or mobility, and any areas you want to protect), your plan, practice history, favourites, reminders and settings are stored only on your phone. TaiChi Flow has no accounts and does not upload this information automatically.

You can erase it at any time in Profile → Reset all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Messages you choose to send

When you use Tell us what you need, we store your message, optional reply email, submission time, app version and platform in a private inbox hosted by Lovable Cloud. Other app users cannot read these messages. No onboarding answers, practice history, purchase identifier or advertising identifier is attached. Please do not include health or payment details.

We use these messages to respond to requests and improve the app, not for advertising. Our hosting provider also processes technical connection information to deliver the request. To request access or deletion, email pranacodes@gmail.com and identify your message.

We retain messages only as long as needed to handle the request and related follow-up. Resetting the app does not delete a message you have already sent.

Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms. We never see your card or payment details.

We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases, unlock TaiChi Flow Plus and restore it on a new device. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID, your purchase and subscription history for this app, the store country and currency, and basic device and app information (model, operating system, app version, IP address). When your privacy choice allows ad measurement, it also receives the advertising identifier and Meta's anonymous app ID so renewals can be matched to our ads.

App usage analytics with PostHog

To understand which parts of the app help people and where they get stuck, the app can send usage events to PostHog (PostHog Inc., servers in the United States): screens opened, onboarding steps reached (not your answers), plans viewed or closed, the reason you pick when closing a plan screen, checkout started, purchases with their plan, price and currency, lessons and meditations started or finished with their length, and the star rating you give.

These events carry a random ID created on your phone, your app language, whether you have TaiChi Flow Plus, the app version, the operating system and your IP address. We never send your onboarding answers, health or mobility details, name or email, and never your advertising identifier.

Usage analytics follow the same privacy choice as ad measurement: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region cannot be determined, nothing is sent unless you choose Allow; elsewhere it is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in Profile → Privacy choices. PostHog processes this data under its own privacy policy (posthog.com/privacy).

Ad measurement with Meta

We advertise TaiChi Flow on Facebook and Instagram. To learn which ads bring people to the app, the app can send these events to Meta: app installed or opened, onboarding finished, first session finished, plans viewed, checkout started, and trial, subscription or one-time purchases with their price and currency.

With these events Meta receives technical data such as device model, operating system, app version, IP address, Meta's anonymous app ID and — only where allowed — your device advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone after you allow tracking; the Google advertising ID on Android). We never send your onboarding answers, health or mobility details, name, email or practice history.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever your region cannot be determined, nothing is sent unless you choose Allow in the privacy sheet. Elsewhere, measurement is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in Profile → Privacy choices. On iPhone, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.

Meta processes this data under its own Data Policy (facebook.com/privacy/policy). You can also limit ad tracking on your device: on iPhone in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android in Settings → Google → Ads (delete or reset the advertising ID).

Notifications, downloads and content updates

Practice reminders are scheduled locally on your phone; no push service or notification token is used.

The first screens, onboarding audio and basic coaching sounds are included in the app. Lesson films, lesson voice, music, voice packs for other app languages and course updates are downloaded from our content storage (Cloudflare R2) when you open a lesson or when the app checks for updates, then kept on your phone so they play offline afterwards. Like any web server, the storage receives your IP address and basic request details to deliver the files; no account, advertising identifier, onboarding answer or practice history is sent with these downloads.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We process purchase information to provide the service you buy (contract), voluntarily submitted support messages to respond and improve our service (legitimate interests), and ad-measurement events and usage analytics where consent is required only on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Profile → Privacy choices. Withdrawing consent stops future sharing; it does not affect sharing that happened before.

Your rights

Your practice history is stored on your phone, so you control it directly; only the usage events described above leave it. For information held by RevenueCat, PostHog or Meta about this app, you can ask us to access or delete it, or object to its use, by emailing us. Include the support code shown in Profile → Privacy choices if you can, so we can find the right records. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Retention and security

On-device data stays until you reset it or uninstall the app. Purchase records are kept by the stores and RevenueCat for as long as required for tax and accounting. Ad-measurement events are kept by Meta and usage analytics by PostHog according to their policies. Data shared with our providers travels over encrypted connections and may be processed outside your country under the providers' safeguards.

Children

TaiChi Flow is made for adults. It is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If we change what is shared or with whom, we will update this policy and ask for your choice again where consent is required.
