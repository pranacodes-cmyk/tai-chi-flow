Support

Last updated 30 October 2026

Need help with TaiChi Flow? Email us and we usually reply within one working day. Tell us your phone model and what happened, and include the support code from Profile → Privacy choices for questions about purchases or data.

Subscriptions

Restore a purchase on a new phone with Profile → TaiChi Flow Plus → Restore purchases, or the Restore link on the plans screen.

Cancel or change a subscription in your App Store account (iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or in Google Play (Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

Practice safely

Every course offers seated or supported options. Stop if anything hurts and check with your doctor before starting if you have a medical condition.
